a container to share opinions that will get you cancelled

workshop

The purpose of the workshop was to create a field where people could share charged truths and stay in embodied presence with difference. We wove music, meditation, and somatic grounding throughout the night - not as performative breaks, but as anchoring technologies to drop us beneath the mind and into the body.

Participants anonymously offered their most controversial or tender “hot takes,” things they rarely say aloud. The group then voted on which specific topics would be worth diving deep into and small discussion groups were formed. In these groups, the invitation was not to convince or negate others, but to share the life experiences, ancestral threads, and cultural conditioning that gave rise to each person's view.

ritual progression

  1. 6:00

    Potluck 🥘

    Everyone contributes one dish

  2. 6:10

    Musical intro 🎵🎻

    Gather in circle for a cello performance

  3. 6:16

    Introduction

    Orientation to intention

    • Why we’re here: to be seen, and share without judgement
    • Guidelines for group
      • Confidentiality
      • Talk less about your opinion, but the specific life experiences you had that led to that opinion
      • Listen to understand, not judge
  4. 6:25

    Meditation 🧘

    Meditation, embodiment, or loving-kindness

  5. 6:35

    Participatory co-creation of controversial themes we’d want to discuss

    “What’s an issue you sometimes struggle to share in public or what’s one of your most controversial takes that would be unpopular?” i.e. I have certain anti-capitalist views like limits on wealth,

    • Everybody writes down their opinion and puts it in a hat
    • Hat gets handed around and each person reads themes out loud
    • Theme gets put into WhatsApp for crowd voting
    • Top 2 voted on topics are subsequent discussion topics
  6. 6:47

    Round #1: Forming Triads

    Randomly assign people into groups of three.

  7. 6:50

    Round #1

    Talk about the 1st issue.

    • What are your perspectives on this topic?
    • What has shaped your perspective to be as it is today?
  8. 7:15

    BREAK

    Change groups

  9. 7:25

    Round #2

    Talk about the 2nd issue.

    • What are your perspectives on this topic?
    • What has shaped your perspective to be as it is today?
  10. 7:50

    Somatic movement 💃

    • Dance, move, loosen up energy
  11. 7:55

    Full-Group Reflections

    Regroup in the circle.

    • What surprised you? What did you enjoy? What was challenging
    • What shifted in your understanding?
  12. 8:30

    Closing checkout

    • 1 word/sentence on emotion/learning

council of the cosmos

workshop

re-enchanting the pursuit of multiple truths

Council of the Cosmos is a structured role-play workshop designed to help participants explore multiple, often conflicting, ways of knowing through embodied perspective-taking.

Each participant was assigned an archetype - such as the Scientist, Mystic, Artist, or Elder - and began by listening to a short audio meditation specific to their role (recorded by yours truly), accessed through QR codes and headphones. These archetypes were drawn from a custom deck that included guiding values, strengths, and blind spots.

calm meditation app

sr. product manager, community

finding pause in community

I was the Sr. Product Manager overseeing community product strategy at Calm. 2 selected projects:

Calm Connect

Calm Connect allows you to start a live session and share it with your friends. Choose any piece of content, and a time you’d like to start. You’ll get a link to share with your friends so you can meditate, relax, or fall asleep together. Afterwards you can reflect with friends on the session.

Calm Groups

Calm Groups enable you to go on a meditation journey with friends over a 1-week or 1-month duration. The feature engenders strong social accountability via sharing progress, reflections, and encouragement along the way.
