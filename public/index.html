patricia mou

is a designer, founder, and researcher working at the intersection of wisdom infrastructure and cultural re-enchantment. I'm currently exploring how novel social forms can help us live with more meaning, coherence, and connection. More on this here.

currently

  • hosting retreats & gatherings for those called to build temples for polyphonic spirituality
  • apprenticing in ritual arts and non-dual meditation teacher training
  • writing and going down rabbit holes
  • co-founder at The Commons
