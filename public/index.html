curriculum design

version 01

the commons campus 🏛️

Our first attempt at programming was 8 weeks of community-created curriculum comprised of 60+ salons, circles, juntos, and containers for play.

  • salons

    Topical deep-dive within a field, research question, interest, problem, or curiosity

  • juntos

    Single-threaded conversations with the same people week over week

  • circles

    Loose support groups for social accountability, and/or feedback & discussion

  • play

    Lighter ways to connect, jam, play, & connect to our inner-child

  • nerd prom

    End of quarter learning retreat to reflect, share, and connect

guilds
mechanism: bonding

Choose a guild for the quarter via the primary way you meaning-make & express:

  • builders

    Builders see problems in the world and tackle them with patience, courage, and insight.

    …if you’re building or birthing something into the world

  • mystics

    Mystics desire to know & experience the deepest wells of wisdom, liberation, and reality in the human condition.

    …if you’re exploring wisdom and spiritual traditions

  • artists

    Artists embody creative energy as their medium of change, expression, and impact.

    …if you’re exploring creative mediums and expression

  • explorers

    Explorers are deeply focused on understanding their inner and outer worlds, in search of discovering something new.

    …if you’re exploring yourself and the world more broadly

🔭 explorer guild programming

The Explorer Guild is a playground to embark on our own Hero’s Journey together. Over 8 weeks, we do a weekly inner and outer challenge together and meet in circles to reflect and integrate.

  1. 🗺️ The Ordinary World

    inner understand our conditioning

    outer go to a place that represents your past

  2. 🔭 Call to Adventure

    inner journal your craziest dreams

    outer outdoor adventure and rituals

  3. 🛑 Refusal of the Call

    inner exploring our self-limiting beliefs

    outer physically “smash” our resistances

  4. 🎢 Trials & Failure

    inner reflect on past fear and failures

    outer do a personal small challenge

  5. 💪 Growth & new skills

    inner inner growth exercises

    outer open mic night

  6. ☠️ Death and Rebirth

    inner journal about a major challenge

    outer do a major challenge

  7. 🙏 Atonement

    inner forgive past selves and others

    outer somatic release activity

  8. ✨ Return with Elixir

    inner reflect on the future you post challenge

    outer create something from this new place

commons rituals

core ritual themes

  • 🧭 Reflect

    Rituals where we remember what truly matters, allowing them to step back from daily tasks and connect with their values.

    eg. Weekly meditation, Weekly Reflections, Sunday Service

  • 🎨 Create

    Rituals that help us make tangible progress on projects, fostering creativity and moving ideas into action individually and collaboratively.

    eg. Builder’s Night, Artist’s Circle, Music Night, Write Night

  • 🪞 Share

    Rituals where participants offer insights into their work, seeking support and collaboration from peers.

    eg. Rabbit-hole-a-thon, Show n Tell

  • 🌀 Commune

    Rituals that create the conditions for open-ended play, wonder, and friendship.

    eg. Movie night, Game night, Authentic Relating

the mystical hearth

sunday service

a sunday service for seekers of the sacred across every path.

The Hearth is the simplest sacred technology: a living center we tend to together. It belongs to no one, yet it warms everyone.

We tend to this center at each service through different portals:

  • co-creation of a collective altar
  • communal meditation
  • a sacred passage and sermon offered by a teacher from rotating wisdom lineages
  • 45 mins of deep reading of a sacred text of one's choosing (however you define sacred; whether that's a textbook, poetry book, or 1,000 year old text)
  • sharing aloud the passages or poems that moved us in our reading

a container to share opinions that will get you cancelled

workshop

The purpose of the workshop was to create a field where people could share charged truths and stay in embodied presence with difference. The invitation was not to convince or negate others, but to share the life experiences, ancestral threads, and cultural conditioning that gave rise to each person's view.

ritual progression

  1. 6:00

    Potluck 🥘

    Everyone contributes one dish

  2. 6:10

    Musical intro 🎵🎻

    Gather in circle for a cello performance

  3. 6:16

    Introduction

    Orientation to intention

    • Why we’re here: to be seen, and share without judgement
    • Guidelines for group
      • Confidentiality
      • Talk less about your opinion, but the specific life experiences you had that led to that opinion
      • Listen to understand, not judge
  4. 6:25

    Meditation 🧘

    Meditation, embodiment, or loving-kindness

  5. 6:35

    Participatory co-creation of controversial themes we’d want to discuss

    “What’s an issue you sometimes struggle to share in public or what’s one of your most controversial takes that would be unpopular?” i.e. I have certain anti-capitalist views like limits on wealth,

    • Everybody writes down their opinion and puts it in a hat
    • Hat gets handed around and each person reads themes out loud
    • Theme gets put into WhatsApp for crowd voting
    • Top 2 voted on topics are subsequent discussion topics
  6. 6:47

    Round #1: Forming Triads

    Randomly assign people into groups of three.

  7. 6:50

    Round #1

    Talk about the 1st issue.

    • What are your perspectives on this topic?
    • What has shaped your perspective to be as it is today?
  8. 7:15

    BREAK

    Change groups

  9. 7:25

    Round #2

    Talk about the 2nd issue.

    • What are your perspectives on this topic?
    • What has shaped your perspective to be as it is today?
  10. 7:50

    Somatic movement 💃

    • Dance, move, loosen up energy
  11. 7:55

    Full-Group Reflections

    Regroup in the circle.

    • What surprised you? What did you enjoy? What was challenging
    • What shifted in your understanding?
  12. 8:30

    Closing checkout

    • 1 word/sentence on emotion/learning
