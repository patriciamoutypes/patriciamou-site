finding pause in community

I was the Sr. Product Manager overseeing community product strategy at Calm. 2 selected projects:

Calm Connect

Calm Connect allows you to start a live session and share it with your friends. Choose any piece of content, and a time you’d like to start. You’ll get a link to share with your friends so you can meditate, relax, or fall asleep together. Afterwards you can reflect with friends on the session.

Calm Groups

Calm Groups enable you to go on a meditation journey with friends over a 1-week or 1-month duration. The feature engenders strong social accountability via sharing progress, reflections, and encouragement along the way.
