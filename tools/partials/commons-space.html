the agora

Inspired by the Athenian Agora and School of Athens, multiple modalities of discussion, play, creation, and exploration enliven the space simultaneously.

  • authentic relating games
  • casual lounging & vibing
  • music jam
  • technical paper discussion
  • book club on Aristotle’s Nicomachean Ethics
  • silent writing / journaling club
  • serendipitous conversation on SF civics
  • collective flourishing salon
  • civics discussion on the abundance agenda
  • robotics demo
  • Pomodoro deep work session
  • navigating life transitions support circle

20 minute gaps are placed between events so that participants can serendipitously mingle with those exploring different topics.
