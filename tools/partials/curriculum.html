the commons campus 🏛️

Our first attempt at programming was 8 weeks of community-created curriculum comprised of 60+ salons, circles, juntos, and containers for play.

  • salons

    Topical deep-dive within a field, research question, interest, problem, or curiosity

  • juntos

    Single-threaded conversations with the same people week over week

  • circles

    Loose support groups for social accountability, and/or feedback & discussion

  • play

    Lighter ways to connect, jam, play, & connect to our inner-child

  • nerd prom

    End of quarter learning retreat to reflect, share, and connect

a sample of the offerings
  • salon

    Opening the American Mind

    with Karthik Bala · 6 weeks

    Stepping out of the present to empathize with classical and traditionalist cultures on their ideas about art, politics, happiness, education, and metaphysics. Can we learn something from the “primitive”?

  • circle

    Artist Sacred Circle

    with Morgan Fabian · 8 weeks

    This group will create a container where our craft takes center stage. We’ll show up for our art by letting our inner child play, forging creative alliances, identifying blockers, & celebrating the gifts each of us bring to the world.

  • circle

    Novel User Interface Design

    with Andy Matuschak · 4 weeks

    A small group for designers inventing novel dynamic media or unusual user interfaces in any modality. We’ll dive deep on each other’s work, offer crit, and jam on ideas.

  • salon

    Metascience Salon Series

    with Anna Wang & Janelle Tam · 4 weeks

    A salon series on utilizing the scientific methodology to study science itself and how the culture of funding science can evolve.

  • junto

    Meaning & Belonging

    with Adi Melamed · 8 weeks

    A simple, single threaded conversation to explore what’s alive in us, our frameworks for meaning-making, and how we foster belonging within ourselves and in the world.

  • salon

    Forming your Inner Compass

    with Logan Ford · 4 weeks

    Helping people discover their true values & beliefs for making decisions with confidence and conviction. We’ll go through well researched interactive exercises & create concrete goals.

  • junto

    Timeless Way of Building

    with Patricia Mou · 6 weeks

    We’ll be reading Christopher Alexander’s “Timeless Way of Building” - philosophy of architecture and urban planning, exploring how people of a society have always pulled the order of their world from their own being.

  • salon

    The how of Why

    with Naim Kabir · 3 weeks

    What does “causation” even mean? Why do we tend to care about it? When we care about it, what do we need to figure out what causes what? A journey through philosophy to hardcore econometrics: the how & why of… well, Why.

  • circle

    Form & Feeling: Poetry Circle

    with Jessica Zhou · 8 weeks

    This is a poetry hangout - where we’ll meander through questions, conversations, and generative prompts focused on guiding people through different orientations to reading and writing poems.

  • play

    Foreign Movie Club

    with James Hennessy · monthly

    We watch one foreign (non western) movie per month and have a discussion on it, while acknowledging cultural and time period context.

version 02

guilds & practicums

guilds
mechanism: bonding

Choose a guild for the quarter via the primary way you meaning-make & express:

  • builders

    Builders see problems in the world and tackle them with patience, courage, and insight.

    …if you’re building or birthing something into the world

  • mystics

    Mystics desire to know & experience the deepest wells of wisdom, liberation, and reality in the human condition.

    …if you’re exploring wisdom and spiritual traditions

  • artists

    Artists embody creative energy as their medium of change, expression, and impact.

    …if you’re exploring creative mediums and expression

  • explorers

    Explorers are deeply focused on understanding their inner and outer worlds, in search of discovering something new.

    …if you’re exploring yourself and the world more broadly

🔭 explorer guild programming

The Explorer Guild is a playground to embark on our own Hero’s Journey together. Over 8 weeks, we do a weekly inner and outer challenge together and meet in circles to reflect and integrate.

  1. 🗺️ The Ordinary World

    inner understand our conditioning

    outer go to a place that represents your past

  2. 🔭 Call to Adventure

    inner journal your craziest dreams

    outer outdoor adventure and rituals

  3. 🛑 Refusal of the Call

    inner exploring our self-limiting beliefs

    outer physically “smash” our resistances

  4. 🎢 Trials & Failure

    inner reflect on past fear and failures

    outer do a personal small challenge

  5. 💪 Growth & new skills

    inner inner growth exercises

    outer open mic night

  6. ☠️ Death and Rebirth

    inner journal about a major challenge

    outer do a major challenge

  7. 🙏 Atonement

    inner forgive past selves and others

    outer somatic release activity

  8. ✨ Return with Elixir

    inner reflect on the future you post challenge

    outer create something from this new place

explorers circle come together to share reflections from challenges in community

  1. Pod check-inscheck-ins to bring us into the present moment
  2. Debriefsharing how our challenges went, giving & receiving advice
  3. Practice sessionsexploring together in community
  4. Integrationsharing intentions for the coming week & renew commitment to exploration
